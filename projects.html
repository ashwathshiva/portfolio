Urban Sound Classification

Deep Learning Environmental Acoustic Event Recognition

An audio machine learning system that accurately identifies and classifies complex environmental sounds in noisy urban acoustic scenes.

Technical Implementation: Converted raw 1D audio waveforms into 2D spatial frequency representations using Librosa to extract 40-band Mel-Frequency Cepstral Coefficients (MFCCs), spectral centroids, and zero-crossing rates. Trained a custom 2D Convolutional Neural Network (CNN) in PyTorch incorporating alternating convolution layers, batch normalization, max pooling, and dropout regularization.

  • Acoustic Decomposition: Extracted 40-band MFCCs and spectrogram representations from raw audio signals
  • 2D CNN Architecture: Built in PyTorch with ReLU activations, batch norm, and spatial max pooling
  • Overfitting Regularization: Applied dropout to reliably generalize across high-variance ambient audio
  • 10-Class Recognition: Categorizes sirens, street music, drilling, engine idling, gunshots, and barking
